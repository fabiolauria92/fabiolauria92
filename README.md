## About me

I'm the CEO & Founder of ELECTE, an AI-powered analytics company I founded in Milan in 2023.

Before ELECTE, I spent more than a decade in technology systems, working across finance, people, customers, and technical operations before becoming CEO. That experience shaped my approach to software: start with the business problem and build tools that help people solve it.

My work spans product direction, company strategy, and the operations behind building a software business. At ELECTE, we're making forecasting, analysis, and reporting accessible to companies without a dedicated data science team.

I also write about what happens when AI becomes part of how businesses operate: who controls the infrastructure, what data reveals beyond what people disclose, and where automation should leave room for human judgment.

## Building ELECTE

[ELECTE](https://www.electe.net) helps growing businesses connect their data, understand performance, and make better-informed decisions.

The platform brings together:

- **Forecasting and predictive analytics** for sales, revenue, and cash flow.
- **Dashboards and reporting** that turn business data into information teams can use.
- **AI-assisted workflows** for analysis, report generation, and competitor research.
- **Integrations, an API, and MCP connectivity** for working with existing tools and AI clients.

ELECTE serves **500+ businesses**, with **80% of turnover from international markets**, according to our [published company figures](https://www.electe.net/who-we-are) as of September 2026.

### [The B+ Trap: How AI Compresses the Creative Spectrum — And What a Rebel Architecture Could Change](https://github.com/fabiolauria92/fabiolauria92/tree/main/research)

This paper examines whether optimization for average human preferences can narrow the creative range of AI outputs. It proposes a Rebel AI framework combining novelty search, adversarial divergent agents, and metacognitive feedback.

## Questions I work on

- **AI adoption:** What makes a model useful enough to change a business decision?
- **Forecasting:** How should teams act when the forecast comes with uncertainty?
- **Privacy and inference:** What can systems learn about people beyond the information they choose to share?
- **Infrastructure and control:** How do vendors, platforms, and technical dependencies shape a company's autonomy?
- **Creativity and judgment:** What happens when systems optimize for the answer most people will accept?
- **Company building:** How can small teams use automation while keeping responsibility clear?
